## Kotter's 8 steps (working notes)

### 1. Create urgency
- Perceived urgency ≠ ground-truth urgency. Validate with the people doing the work before claiming a "burning platform".
- Lever: data rather than top-down narrative.

### 2. Build a guiding coalition
- Composition: a sponsor with authority + a few credible operators + at least one frontline champion.
- Anti-pattern: a top-down-only coalition with no frontline voice → silent resistance.

### 3. Form a strategic vision
- A vision in one sentence, testable. If you can't say it in one sentence, it isn't clear yet.
- Link it explicitly to the broader strategy.

### 4. Enlist a volunteer army
- Aim for ~20% of people engaged voluntarily before pushing the other 80%.
- No mass announcement before the coalition + champions are ready.

### 5. Enable action by removing barriers
- The top barrier is usually tooling/process, not motivation.
- If the barrier isn't removed, training and incentives won't move the needle.

### 6. Generate short-term wins
- A 30-60-90 day plan with visible, measurable wins.
- Anti-pattern: announcing a win prematurely (it costs credibility for the next ones).

### 7. Sustain acceleration
- Risk: the enthusiasm phase fades around month 3. Plan an explicit re-launch.
- Recognize early adopters individually.

### 8. Institute change
- Update process docs, onboard new joiners, set durable metrics.
- Decisive test: a new joiner 6 months later doesn't even know there was a change → success.

## Observed anti-patterns

[Enrich as projects and lessons accumulate.]

- **Big-bang rollout** with no pilot → high rejection rate.
- **Training only** with no tooling change → operator frustration.
- **Centralization without local input** → political backlash (cf. historical decisions to log in 05-decisions/).
